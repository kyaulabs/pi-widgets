# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project free from harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act in ways that support an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected conduct

Examples of acceptable behavior include:

- treating other people with empathy and respect;
- accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility for mistakes and repairing their effects; and
- focusing discussion on the project's technical and community needs.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language, imagery, attention, or advances;
- trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or personal and political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without permission; and
- conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this policy. They may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior to [git+abuse@kyaulabs.com](mailto:git+abuse@kyaulabs.com). Maintainers will review reports promptly and fairly. They will protect the reporter's privacy and security as far as the investigation permits.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the project in public, including through an official email address or social media account.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers use the following guidelines when deciding a response.

### Correction

For inappropriate language or other unprofessional conduct, maintainers may issue a private warning that explains the violation and required correction. They may request a public apology.

### Warning

For a single incident or a series of violations, maintainers may prohibit contact with the people involved for a defined period. Ignoring the restriction may result in a temporary or permanent ban.

### Temporary ban

For a serious or sustained violation, maintainers may prohibit all project interaction for a defined period. Ignoring the restriction may result in a permanent ban.

### Permanent ban

For a pattern of violations, harassment, aggression, or disparagement of a group, maintainers may permanently prohibit public interaction with the project.
